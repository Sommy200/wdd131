1. Site Name

DE-IYKE Swimming Club

The name identifies the club directly, so visitors know right away who runs the site and what it is about. It is short and easy to remember, and the words "Swimming Club" make the activity clear in search results. The logo (shown above) uses water waves in the club's blue and a small orange buoy to match the color scheme.

Optional domain: de-iyke-swim.org

2. Site Purpose

The site is a hub for current members and prospective swimmers. It introduces the club's mission, history, coaches, and achievements; describes programs for beginners, competitive youth, adults, and water safety; lists practice times, pricing tiers, and how to join; and gives contact details and location. A gallery/events page may show recent meets and community swim days.

Planned pages

  • Home: club introduction, mission, and a welcoming hero image of the pool.
  • About: history, coaching staff, and achievements.
  • Programs: beginner lessons, competitive youth training, adult fitness swimming, and water safety classes.
  • Schedule/Membership: practice times, pricing, and how to join.
  • Contact: location, hours, and a contact form.
  • Gallery/Events (optional): photos from meets and community days.

3. Scenarios

Questions a typical visitor might ask, which the content will answer:

  1. What swim program is right for my 7-year-old who cannot swim yet?
  2. When are practices held, and how much does membership cost?
  3. Who are the coaches, and what competitions has the club won?
  4. Do you offer adult fitness swimming or water safety classes?
  5. Where is the pool, and how can I contact the club to try a session?
