About us

Our story

DE-IYKE Swimming Club began as a small group of neighbors who wanted safe, affordable swim lessons for their children. Today we welcome families, young competitors and adults, and we still believe that everyone deserves to learn to swim.

Our mission is simple: build confidence, fitness and water safety skills in a friendly community.
